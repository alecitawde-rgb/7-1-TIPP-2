Reading: What Could Our World Look Like in 2050?

Original classroom adaptation based on Laura Cress, “AI teachers and cybernetics – what could the world look like in 2050?”, BBC, 2 January 2026. These are experts’ predictions, not established facts.

Humans and machines

By 2050, tiny technologies called nanotechnology may help doctors deliver medicine to specific parts of the body. Electronic implants might help people monitor their health or communicate.

Quantum computers and AI

Quantum computers could help scientists solve very complex problems, such as designing new medicines. Artificial intelligence will probably continue to influence many parts of our lives.

Schools in 2050

Some experts predict that students may learn with AI teachers. These systems could adapt lessons to different students. Instead of using only textbooks, learners might explore subjects through immersive virtual simulations.

Transport and space

Driverless cars could make roads safer and reduce traffic congestion. People may also live and work at a base on the Moon.

Science fiction or reality?

Science fiction has imagined futuristic technologies for many years. Some predictions may become real, but others might remain fiction. How we use technology will matter.

2 · Grammar detective

Read the chart. Then explain the rule to your teacher or partner.

FormMeaningExample
will + base verbPrediction (often more confident)AI will influence our lives.
may + base verbPossibilityImplants may help patients.
might + base verbPossibilityStudents might use simulations.
could + base verbPossible resultCars could reduce congestion.

May, might and could overlap in meaning; they do not form a fixed probability scale.

Explain it: What form of the verb follows these modals? What is the difference between a prediction and a possibility?

3 · Science fiction meets science

In The Machine Stops (1909), E. M. Forster imagines people who depend on a Machine for communication and daily life. In the BBC article, experts imagine AI teachers and virtual simulations.

Think: Could AI make education more accessible? Might it also make people more isolated? Give one benefit and one risk.

There is no single correct answer. Use evidence from both texts.

4 · Puerto Madryn 2050: Tech consultant challenge

Model: A school wants to save energy. Smart sensors could turn lights off when rooms are empty. This may reduce electricity use. However, the school will need to protect the data collected by its devices.

Choose two technologies from the BBC reading. Explain how each could be used in Puerto Madryn. Include two predictions, one advantage and one possible problem.
